## Summary A

This is a Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE) between YayYo, Inc., a Delaware corporation, and an investor, offered privately under Regulation D and Regulation S as part of a $5,000,000 SAFE Preferred Stock offering ending June 30, 2017. Payment is due within two business days, and the Purchase Amount accrues 8% annual dividends. Upon an Equity Financing (the Regulation A+ offering or an IPO), the SAFE converts to common stock at a discount of 50% to 10%, depending on the Sale Date. A Liquidity Event lets the investor choose cash or shares, and a Dissolution Event pays the investor ahead of other stockholders. If no such event occurs by December 31, 2017, the SAFE force-converts, subject to a $50 million valuation cap. The agreement also contains securities-law representations, accredited-investor and anti-money-laundering certifications, California governing law, and a jury-trial waiver.
